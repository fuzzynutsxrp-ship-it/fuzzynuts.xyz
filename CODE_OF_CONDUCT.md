# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Positive behaviour: empathy, respectful disagreement, accepting constructive
feedback, focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour: harassment, trolling, insulting comments, personal
or political attacks, publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to **conduct@fuzzynuts.xyz**. Reports are
reviewed within 72 hours. Enforcement uses the Contributor Covenant
[Enforcement Ladder](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement):
correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members
of the project's leadership.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions,
chat) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
