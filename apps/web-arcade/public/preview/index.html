Play with friends

Grab a friend and play on the same keyboard or set up a private room to play online from anywhere. Compete against players in Nut Royale, or pass the keyboard for a match of Fuzzy Soccer. No waiting, no matchmaking, just instant multiplayer chaos.

Challenge yourself

Do you have what it takes to take on the most challenging games on FuzzyNuts? Test your driving skills in Fuzzy Kart, survive the permadeath dungeons of Dungeon Nuts, or see how far you can run in Sky Nuts. Every run is about staying in control when things get messy.

Games to relax

Enjoy playing games where you can take your time and unwind. Let your creativity flourish in Squirrel Tycoon, manage your crops in Fuzzy Farm, or solve physics puzzles in Acorn Builder. Zero stress, no timers, just cozy gameplay at your own pace.
